A bit about me

I'm a Computer Science grad from the University of Manitoba who landed in cybersecurity through co-ops and stuck around. Day to day, I write detection logic for Microsoft Sentinel, hunt for noisy alerts, and automate the boring parts of incident response with Logic Apps.

Outside the SOC, I ship things. Waifu Music serves voice to over 1,400 Discord servers, LobbyEZ turns Discord into temporary voice rooms with shareable codes, and the site you're reading runs on a small server under my desk — protected by Cloudflare and scripted to recover after every power blip.
